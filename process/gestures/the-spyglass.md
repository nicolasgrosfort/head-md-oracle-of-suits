# The Spyglass

Both index fingers meet, then separate to zoom into the scene.
As the fingers move apart, the view zooms in, narrowing the field of vision.
When the hands move closer to the screen, the focus sharpens, revealing hidden details.
The player can move the spyglass freely through space to explore the environment.
